## My first PRs 

Building off of the tips I've been given from the contributors meeting, I have been in contact with another contributor Tamara via the Slack channel. All the notes I took during the meeting were already added to our group page, including the post Joris shared regarding GFI. This week in class we dedicated time to going across different documentation pages for small fixes.

I will note that the one change I have noticed is community really drives change. By this I mean, the confirmation that I am working with real people who are welcoming and accepting of help makes it easier to lock in and readily contribute. 

As a result, most meetings do seem redundant unless to talk more about the progress we've made. That doesn't mean I deem them totally irrelevant, but they've become shorter as we've just sort of talked about our own PRs. I wouldn't be surprised if they got longer once we start working on harder issues. As for my PR, I have noted that the tests do take a while so I will go back and identify where my faults lie. Besides that, I was luckily caught up on the last Pandas meeting with Tamara. Since there will be some back up in PRs, I think its important to not let that affect the morale of the work we are doing.
