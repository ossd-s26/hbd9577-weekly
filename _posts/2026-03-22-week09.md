This week I attended the Pandas New Contributor's meeting. It was very insightful in the way that it gave me a better feel for the community. I met two other new collaborators and we discussed our questions and experiences with one of the main moderators named Joris. In a way, it kind of felt like a coffee chat. There was time dedicated to personally finding issues to work on with Joris. Per his advice, instead of just looking at good first issues we need to be patient and see if we can identify any issues ourselves first. This included scouring documentation pages for any errors in formatting or explanations. One of the suggested issues to work on was a formatting error in the DataFrame page. Per his advice, instead of reporting it as an issue, it would be best to just make the PR for it since it is relatively small.

As I've been working on the aforementioned PR, I also felt there was slight vagueness in the explanations for document setup. Tamara, another new collaborator, agreed with me. I'm interested in possibly revamping the page so that it is slightly more clear.
