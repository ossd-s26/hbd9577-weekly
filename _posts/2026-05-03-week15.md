My group has resolved to split each required group question so that everyone gets a chance to present besides just our PRs. I am in charge of introducing Pandas and the community, since I had the most interactions with them outside of Github comments. I will say it is very nervewracking, since public presentations are not really my favorite. I am still working further on my datetime bug/enhancement and have it as a draft PR so that I do not forget where it lies. Something I've noticed is that if I do not make explicit note of it, I can start to forget important details to keep in mind about my PR attempts.


I resonated most with the presentation on Affine. Specifically, the mention of spending so much time browsing good issues to make. In my experience, I am not really able to search Pandas and find any good bugs myself since I have not really used it before. So, most of my contributions would also have to be based on monitoring the issues that are at least a couple of months old. It also gets really easy to feel intimidated when starting in open source, as some people seem to have no problem getting their environment set up and their hands dirty immediately (cough cough Nanobot). Hearing the Affine group members talk about how some of them needed time to adjust to the codebase and figure out how to get rid of a bug was very affirming.
